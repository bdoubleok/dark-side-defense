Sources & Definitions

  • Pass rush win rate: ESPN Analytics, . Team figure plus the top 20 edge rushers and top 20 defensive tackles only.
  • Pressures, hurries, QB hits, sacks, times blitzed (2026 players and team totals): Pro Football Reference advanced defense, via nflverse. Pressures are credited to players, so two rushers arriving on one play count twice.
  • Blitz rate and rusher count: FTN charting via nflverse. Blitz = at least one charted blitzer on the dropback.
  • QB contact rate: sack or QB hit on the dropback, from nflverse play-by-play. A subset of pressure, since hurries are not in play-by-play.
  • Pressure rate, 2025: play-level pressure flag from nflverse participation data, available for 2025 only. Not comparable with the 2026 player-credited figure.
  • 2026 pressure by quarter and down, coverage, rusher count, personnel, and individual rush snaps and wins: SümerBrain (Sumer Sports), supplied from the project owner's account. Provider-reported and not audited play by play. Pressure is counted once per dropback. Its dropback and sack counts match nflverse play-by-play in every quarter and every down of every game, and its season splits each add up to the same 318 dropbacks and 126 pressures.
  • Dropbacks: pass attempts, sacks and scrambles against Seattle, excluding spikes and two-point tries. Overtime is folded into Q4.
