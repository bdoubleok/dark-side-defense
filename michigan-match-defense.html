Section 5: User Stick Execution & Hit-Stick Triggers

Stick Mechanics

3Rec Hook (Linebacker)

Pre-Snap: Mug the A/B gap to threaten interior pass protection.
Post-Snap: Take 2 read steps in strafe (LT/L2). Squeeze the 1st shallow crosser entering your window.
The Handoff: Deliver shallow route to flat defender, snap eyes inside to rob the 2nd crosser (dig/bender).
Hit-Stick: Flick Right Stick DOWN (Cut-Tackle) to eliminate forward YAC lean.

Weak Safety (Diesel Poach)

Pre-Snap: Hold balanced alignment on hash at 10–12 yds. Never cheat early.
Post-Snap: Key #3 receiver release. If vertical/cross, run an angled top-down diagonal track.
The Interception: Maintain a 2-yd cushion above the route; drive downhill when QB front shoulder unlocks.
Hit-Stick: Flick Right Stick UP (Truck-Stopper) to dislodge ball at catch point.
