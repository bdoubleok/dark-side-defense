# DARK SIDE DEFENSE v3 — Canonical Build

**Mike Macdonald / 2022 Michigan (Minter) Scheme · CFB ’26**

> Canonical merge of Dark Side v1 (7-formation streamlined), v2 (10-formation full), and the corrected build. Architecturally pivoted: 4-down nickel base via the **4-2-5 playbook**. Every formation and play verified against [cfb.fan](https://cfb.fan/26/playbooks/) as of May 2026.

-----

## Why This Version Exists

The earlier Dark Side builds picked **3-3-5** as the base template and explicitly skipped 4-2-5 / 4-3 Multiple with this note: *“Their in-game play menus are missing sim pressures, Cover 1 Robber, and edge blitz concepts.”*

The cfb.fan audit shows that was wrong. **Nickel Over (native to the 4-2-5 playbook)** carries the most complete Macdonald-friendly menu in the entire game:

- Every coverage: Match, Buzz Mable, Sky, Quarters, Palms, Cover 6 / 6 Willie, Cover 9 Show 2, Tampa 2, Cover 1 Hole
- Every signature pressure: **Sim Pressure 3**, **Edge Blitz 3**, **Field Sim 3** (new ’26), **Nickel Sim 2** (new ’26)
- New CFB ’26 additions: Bracket Switch Willie, Double Bracket Switch, 1 Double Slot, 1 Double TE

v3 corrects the architecture and merges the v1 streamlined build, v2 full build, and the master call sheet into one document.

## Identity

*Front philosophy*

- Four-down nickel first.
- 4-2-5 fronts and Mug looks are presentation, not automatic pressure.
- Mint and Over Flex are change-up shells against spread.

*Coverage philosophy*

- Two-high match coverage first.
- Live in Quarters, Palms, Cover 6, and Cover 3 Match / Buzz.
- Tampa 2 and Drop calls are situational answers.

*Pressure philosophy*

- Stunts and sims over true blitz.
- Pressure spike on 3rd & 7+. Short yardage and red zone are the other windows.
- Real-Macdonald math: ~19-22% blitz rate, ~36% pressure rate.

**Playbook allocation: 65% base coverage / 25% sims & stunts / 10% true blitz.**

## The Hybrid Coverage Token System

**Core principle (design-tokens analogy).** Every formation has *exactly one* play in each of the four core token slots: Danger, 3 Lock, Pound, Stuff. Calling “Pound” from any formation always returns the same coverage role (2-high quarters match). You learn four *concepts*, not 40 plays.

**Specialty slots** = 2–4 plays unique to each formation that don’t fit the core tokens but are too good to skip.

### Core Tokens

|Token     |Coverage Family                                  |Pre-Snap Look|Best Against                    |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------|-------------|--------------------------------|
|**DANGER**|Cover 3 Match family (1-high pattern match)      |1-high       |Standard downs, balanced offense|
|**3 LOCK**|Cover 3 Buzz Mable / Cover 3 Sky (1-high press)  |1-high press |Quick game, timing routes       |
|**POUND** |Cover 4 Quarters / Cover 4 Palms (2-high match)  |2-high       |Spread, 3rd & long, empty       |
|**STUFF** |Cover 6 / 6 Willie / 6 Invert (2-high asymmetric)|2-high       |Trips, red zone, disguise       |

### Secondary Tokens

|Token         |Coverage                                      |Role                                              |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
|**CLOUD**     |Cover 2 Invert                                |Strong safety in box, FS over top — change-of-pace|
|**SQUAT**     |Cover 4 Palms vs Bunch (with adjustments)     |Bunch specialist — distribute by area             |
|**SAND DEUCE**|Edge Blitz 3                                  |Disguised 5-man pressure + Cover 2 shell          |
|**ROBBER**    |Cover 1 Robber / Cover 1 Hole / 1 Robber Press|Man-free with reading defender — red zone weapon  |
|**SHOW 2**    |Cover 9 Show 2                                |Man-free disguised as 2-high shell                |

### Golden Rules

- **Never show the same look twice in a row.**
- **Alternate 1-high (Danger / 3 Lock) and 2-high (Pound / Stuff / Cloud)** every play if possible.
- **Mix in Sand Deuce every 4-5 plays.**
- **Bunch = Squat. Always.**
- **Red zone = Stuff or Squat.**
- **Hurry-up = Simplify to Danger + Pound only.**
- **Make the QB think every single play.**

## Pre-Snap Adjustment Recipes

These five recipes turn ~45 base plays into 70+ distinct looks. Master Recipe 1 first.

### Recipe 1 — Mugged A-Gap Disguise

**Steps.** Call any zone play from Nickel Over or Mint → Double-tap RB/R1 (Show Blitz, LBs only) → Both LBs walk to A-gaps → Coverage plays as called post-snap.

**Best with.** `Cover 3 Buzz Mable`, `Cover 3 Match`, `Tampa 2`.

**Why.** Offense sees blitz, blocks for pressure, you drop 7 into coverage. Macdonald’s signature deception.

### Recipe 2 — Sim Pressure Enhancement

**Steps.** Call `Sim Pressure 3` or `Field Sim 3` → Pinch DL → Spread LBs → Let the designed rush exchange unfold.

**Best with.** Any Sim Pressure variant.

**Why.** Pinched DL creates interior confusion while the LB/DL swap generates a free rusher.

### Recipe 3 — Custom Robber from Cover 3

**Steps.** Call `Cover 3 Sky` → Select strong safety (A/X) → Hot route to Hook Zone (left stick up) → Use Custom Zones (hold LB/L1) to drag drop spot to 8-10 yard depth over the ball.

**Best with.** `Cover 3 Sky`, `Cover 3 Match`.

**Why.** Creates a de facto robber sitting in the intermediate middle. Best when you user-control the safety.

### Recipe 4 — Hot Blitz Bail Optimization

**Steps.** Call `Hot Blitz Bail` from Nickel 3-3 Mint → Turn Auto Flip OFF → Flip play call → Shift DL Left → Spread LBs → Zone out slot CB and left edge into curl flats.

**Best with.** `Hot Blitz Bail` only.

**Why.** Looping MLB comes free through the A-gap. Community’s highest-rated CFB ’26 blitz setup.

### Recipe 5 — Coverage Shell Disguise

**Steps.** Call `Cover 3 Buzz Mable` (naturally shows two-high pre-snap) → Shade coverage down to bring buzz defender closer to the line.

**Best with.** `Cover 3 Buzz Mable`.

**Why.** Pre-snap looks like Cover 2 or Quarters. Post-snap it’s Cover 3 with a robber. QB reads the wrong coverage.

## Coverage Rotation Principles

Macdonald’s system is designed to cycle between coverages so the QB can never settle into a rhythm.

**The 1-High / 2-High Oscillation (per play).**

- Play 1: DANGER (1-high) → QB reads single-high safety
- Play 2: POUND (2-high) → QB reads split safeties
- Play 3: 3 LOCK (1-high) → QB thinks it’s Danger, but corners are pressing
- Play 4: STUFF (2-high) → QB thinks it’s Pound, but one side is Cover 2

**Series-Based Rotation (if cycling every play is too aggressive).**

- Series 1: DANGER-heavy (70/30 with 3 LOCK). Establish base.
- Series 2: POUND-heavy (60/40 with STUFF). Shift to 2-high.
- Series 3: Mix everything. No pattern for the offense.

**Constraint Rotation (pair coverage to what the offense is doing).**

- Quick game (slants, hitches, screens) → 3 LOCK. Press disrupts timing.
- Intermediate (digs, crossers, posts) → DANGER. 4-under structure walls off the middle.
- Deep (go, post, corner) → POUND. Four deep defenders.
- Schemed to one side (trips, flood) → STUFF. Site the halves to the strength.

**Pairing rules (if/then sequencing).**

- After a completion of 15+ yards → POUND or STUFF next. Protect deep.
- After a sack or TFL → 3 LOCK. Press them while they’re on their heels.
- After a quick pass for 5+ → 3 LOCK. Disrupt the timing.
- After a run for 5+ → DANGER. Get the 4th underneath defender in the box.
- After offensive penalty → POUND. Make them earn every yard.
- 3rd down after two runs → STUFF. Force the QB to diagnose.
- After offense calls timeout → CHANGE the coverage. Whatever you showed pre-timeout, call something different.

## Core Philosophy Cheat Sheet

> **Never settle into a pattern.** Show two-high, play Cover 3 Buzz. Show blitz, drop Tampa 2. Show man press, rotate Cover 6. The scheme’s power is uncertainty.

> **Pressure without blitzing.** Macdonald’s defenses blitz on ~19-22% of plays and generate ~36% pressure rate. Use sim pressures and stunts as primary pass rush.

> **Four plays, four looks.** `Cover 3 Buzz Mable`, `Cover 3 Match`, `Cover 4 Palms`, and `Sim Pressure 3` should be ~60% of your playcalls. Master these four before adding complexity.

> **The mug is a weapon.** Nickel Double Mug with Show Blitz forces the offense to keep extra blockers in. Even if you never blitz from it, the threat changes their math.

> **User the robber.** Cover 1 Robber is exponentially better when you control the safety. Read the QB’s eyes; jump the crosser. This is your competitive edge.

> **Recruit the Emmanwori.** A 6’2”, 220-lb safety who can tackle, cover, and play in the box is the single most important position on the defense.
